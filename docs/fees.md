# Fees and refunds
## Tuition
Tuition for the full programme is 45,000 taka. It can be paid at once or in three equal instalments.
## Refund policy
Learners who withdraw within the first 14 days receive a full refund. After 14 days, no tuition refund is available.
## Scholarships
Two merit scholarships covering half of the tuition are awarded each cohort, based on the screening test score.
